Welcome

Feel Right at
Home in Tezpur

Village Vista Homestay is one of Tezpur's most loved guesthouses — and it's easy to see why. Tucked away in a quiet, well-connected part of town, our property feels less like a hotel and more like staying with family. The lush garden, relaxing terrace, and spotlessly clean rooms create an atmosphere that guests consistently describe as homely, cozy, and far better than a star hotel.

Whether you're a couple seeking a peaceful retreat, a family exploring Assam, or a traveller on the way to Kaziranga — we'll make sure you feel welcome, comfortable, and cared for every single day. Bed linen, towels, free Wi-Fi, and free private parking are all included.
